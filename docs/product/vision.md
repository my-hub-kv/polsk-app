# Product vision

Polsk App is a private, invitation-only coordination app for one recurring yearly family event of roughly 40–50 people, including adults and children. It reduces friction in chore planning, channel-based communication, a shared agenda, and food, shopping, dinner-box, and vendor-export coordination.

> Open the app, see today, understand what is happening, and know whether you need to do anything.

The product is family-oriented, friendly, calm, mobile-first, clear, forgiving, and low-friction. Administrative work may be complex when necessary, but that complexity must not leak into the ordinary participant experience. The name came from an internal typo and has no connection to Poland.
