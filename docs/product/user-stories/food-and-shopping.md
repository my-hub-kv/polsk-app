# Food and shopping user stories

Status: Confirmed

## FOD-01: Understand food availability

As a participant, I want to see basic and reserved food, including dinner-box reservations, so that I do not mistake reserved stock for free stock.

### Acceptance criteria

Adults can report missing items; others see the reported state; food coordinators verify, correct, reverse, and record approximate or optional counted stock.

### Authorization

Only authorised coordinators verify stock.

### Important edge cases

Reported missing is distinct from a verified count.

### Out of scope

Precise consumption ledger.

## FOD-02: Request and export shopping

As a participant, I want to submit a request, and as a coordinator I want to edit, merge, suggest, organise, export, and track shopping lists, so that deliveries are practical.

### Acceptance criteria

Suggestions show reasons, remain editable, use delivery days, export CSV safely, retain export history, warn on later changes, and support operational list status.

### Authorization

Participants submit; coordinators edit, verify, and export.

### Important edge cases

Historical purchases and weather are contextual, not automatic decisions.

### Out of scope

Automatic ordering, budgets, receipts, barcode scanning, and shopper assignment.
