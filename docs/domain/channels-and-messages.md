# Channels and messages

**Product rules: Confirmed. Domain design: Candidate. Implementation: Planned.**

Public channels are discoverable by participants and have voluntary subscription; official channels are administrator-designated, subscribed by default, and cannot be muted; private channels are administrator-created and visible only to members. Every active event year has at least one official all-participants channel. Subscription controls notification, never visibility.

Messages are event-year scoped. Owners edit/delete their own messages; administrators may delete any message. Private-channel queries enforce object-level membership and must not reveal ordinary existence to non-members. Retention follows policy; there is no seen workflow or direct messaging in v1.
